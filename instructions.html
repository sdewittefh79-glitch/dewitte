About the Game


Target Audience - Corporate leaders participating in a company-run leadership development training conference


Details and Instructions - This activity is intended to be run during breaks as a way to engage participants and informally assess Level 2 knowledge. Participants are arranged in table groups of 4-6. Facilitators will click the button to spin the wheel, which will randomly land on a number ranging from 1-8. The table group that is assigned the number where the wheel lands is given first opportunity to answer a Level 2 knowledge question related to previously-learned content. If the group answers correctly, they earn a group team-building prize. Prize options include a company-sponsored happy hour at a 5-star restaurant and a trip to their local virtual reality-based radio control car race track called RC Madness. If the team with the corresponding wheel number does not answer correctly, the facilitator will spin the wheel again, allowing another team to answer the same question. If the re-spin lands on the same number as that of the first team who did not answer correctly, the facilitator spins again. The game continues in this manner until a team answers correctly. Click Here to see a sample question.


How it was Built - Simple HTML and CSS were used for structure and styling, respectively. A container div element provided the basic wheel structure. For CSS, pseudo classes were used to enhance interactivity and the clip-path polygon property was used to style the wheel. For Javascript, a combination of: let variables, click-button function, and math-ceiling method were built to activate starts-on-button-click and random number stops. Much of my inspiration came from this Youtube video: https://youtu.be/WPVFggcDeMI?si=oMfl2HhfE-BxhOQZ. The Happy Hour and RC Madness question graphics were created in illustrator.
